    WORLD & CHARACTER CONSISTENCY

    Canon Vault.

    Production anchors from your established worldbuilding. These are brief reference cards, not replacements for the complete chapter manuscripts.

    Authority order: Explicit new author revisions (where supplied) → published chapters → locked canon notes → provisional ideas. A missing detail is unknown, not permission to invent it.

    Continuity safeguards

    No unapproved costume, weapon, ability, outcome or relationship changes
    Separate published information from inferred staging choices
    Maintain per-shot position, injuries, equipment, lighting and voice direction
    Use source passages for exact dialogue and sequence fidelity

    V2 adds a manuscript library, offline targeted checks, and optional AI-assisted review. Full semantic verification depends on the supplied chapters; never treat AI output as automatically authoritative.
